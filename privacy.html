Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What magx accesses

With your permission, magx uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets. It reads the metadata (name, modification time) of documents linked in your agent sessions and exports their content (as .docx, .xlsx or Markdown). It can also edit a document when you explicitly ask an agent to do so.

Where the data goes

All data stays on your own computer: exported snapshots and extracted text are stored in a local folder (~/.magx) and in a local database. magx has no servers. It does not send your Google data to the author or to any third party, does not sell it and does not use it for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally in encrypted form by the Google Workspace CLI (gws).

Google API Services User Data Policy

magx's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke magx's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local data is removed by deleting the ~/.magx folder.
